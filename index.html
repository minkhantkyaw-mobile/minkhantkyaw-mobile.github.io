About

Building real systems for real businesses

Client and in-house products across Myanmar — POS, ERP, laundry, loyalty, and more.

I am a mobile developer focused on Flutter for Android, iOS, tablet, and desktop. Most of my work is production software for businesses: restaurant POS, accounting + POS (ERP), fuel and inventory systems, laundry logistics, loyalty apps, and e-commerce.

I care about clean architecture, solid state management (GetX, Riverpod, Bloc, Provider), and UI that holds up on phones, tablets, and Windows desktops. I have shipped apps through Google Play and the Apple App Store.

I am also growing my backend skills with Laravel so I can contribute across the full product lifecycle — from API to release.
